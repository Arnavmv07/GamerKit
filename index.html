New PC setup · Windows 10 / 11
Press a key, or type GAME… on your keyboard, to change the lighting.

Every app a fresh gaming PC needs, in one place. Pick your launchers, drivers and tools, then download each from its official site or install the whole kit with one command.

Start from a build

Each preset adds a ready-made set of tools to your kit. You can add or remove anything after.

step 1

Build your kit

Tap + on any tool or start from a preset. Your kit is saved in this browser.

step 2

Install in one go

Open your kit and copy the winget command into PowerShell. It installs every supported app silently, one after another.

step 3

Grab the rest

Drivers and vendor apps without a winget package get a direct button to the official download page.

Gamerkit hosts no files. Every Download button opens the developer's own site, and the winget command pulls packages from Microsoft's community repository.

Product names belong to their owners. Check a tool's licence before you install it.
